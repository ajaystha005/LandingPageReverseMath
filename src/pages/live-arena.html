Live Arena: a multiplayer math game to play with friends

Live Arena is the multiplayer math game in ReverseMath. You and your friends play the same puzzles at the same time, and you can watch who is in the lead while you play.

How a Live Arena works

  1. Open a Live Arena. Pick a level and you get a code and a link.
  2. Share it. Friends join the room with the code or the link.
  3. Start. The host starts the game when two or more players are in.
  4. Race. Everyone gets the same puzzles at the same time. You see who is leading as you go.
  5. Compare. When the round ends, you see how everyone did.

A Live Arena round lasts 2 minutes, so one game does not take long.

Live Arena or Challenge Battle?

Both are ways to play your friends, and both use a code or link.

  • Live Arena: everyone plays together, right now. It is the one to pick when your friends are around.
  • Challenge Battle: you send a code, and friends play the same puzzles later, at their own pace.

Tips to win

  • Read the target and find the × and ÷ signs first.
  • Skim the target and the signs first, then place your numbers.
  • Watch the standings, but keep your eyes on your own puzzle.
  • Want to practise first? Race the clock alone in Survival.
