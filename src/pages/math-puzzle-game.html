ReverseMath: a free math puzzle game

ReverseMath is a free math puzzle game for iPhone and Android. You see a target number and an equation with blanks. Your job is to place the numbers so the equation comes out to the target. It is a number puzzle that is quick to start, and a hard one if you want it to be.

What is a math puzzle game?

A math puzzle game turns arithmetic into a puzzle. You do not just work out an answer. You look for a way to make the numbers fit. Think of it as a small brain puzzle you can play anywhere.

What makes ReverseMath different

  • You work backwards. It is a reverse math puzzle: you get the answer first, and you find the way to it. How a reverse math puzzle works
  • Real math rules. Multiplying and dividing come before adding and subtracting, just like in class.
  • Every number counts. You use each number once.
  • Quick to start. There is no sign-up, and the first level is gentle.

Pick your pace

  • Daily: five new puzzles every day, the same for everyone. Daily math puzzle
  • Classic: relaxed play with four levels, Novice, Seasoned, Expert and Genius.
  • Survival: beat the clock and earn time with each puzzle you solve. Survival mode
  • Live Arena: play the same puzzles together, live. Live Arena
  • Challenge Battle: send a code and friends play at their own pace.

Who it is for

ReverseMath is for anyone who likes numbers and puzzles: people who do a puzzle every morning, people who like to beat a friend's score, and people who like a quick bit of mental math. You do not need to be a math expert. The first level is gentle, and Undo is there in every mode.

It is free on iPhone and Android, and you can also play in your browser.
