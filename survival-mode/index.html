Survival mode: a math game against the clock

Survival is the ReverseMath mode where the clock is the boss. You start with a short timer. Every puzzle you solve earns time back. When the time runs out, your run is over. The question is simple: how many puzzles can you survive?

How Survival works

  • You start with a short countdown.
  • Every puzzle you solve adds time to the clock.
  • A wrong answer costs you a few seconds.
  • When the clock reaches zero, the run ends, and you see how many puzzles you survived.

Survival keeps your best run and shows how close you are to a new one, so there is always something to beat.

Puzzles get harder as you go

The first puzzles are gentle. Then the levels climb. Harder puzzles earn you more time, so a tough one is a risk that can pay off.

If you are new to the idea, read how to play first: you fill each number into a blank so the equation hits the target.

Tips to last longer

  • Do not guess. A wrong answer costs time, and a careful answer is usually faster than two guesses.
  • Look for the × and ÷ signs first. They do the heavy work.
  • Use Undo if you place a number and change your mind.
  • Stay calm when the clock is low. Take the puzzle you can solve fastest.

Survival or Classic?

Survival is a race against the clock. If you would rather take your time, play Classic, with four levels from Novice to Genius and no countdown. For a head-to-head race, try a Live Arena.
