Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

Every puzzle is a short equation with blanks. Place each number once so the answer is the target. It is a reverse math puzzle: you start from the target and work out where the numbers go.

  1. 1 Fill the blanks

    Drag or tap each number into a blank. Use every number once.

  2. 2 Real math rules

    × and ÷ are worked out before + and −. So the order matters.

  3. 3 Hit the target

    Green check, next puzzle. Wrong? Swap the numbers and try again.

Quick answers

What is ReverseMath?

A free math puzzle game. You see a target number and an equation with blanks, and you place the given numbers so the equation comes out to the target.

Is ReverseMath free?

Yes. It is free on iPhone and Android.
