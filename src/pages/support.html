Support

Help with the ReverseMath math puzzle game: the Daily, Classic, Survival, Live Arena, Challenge Battle, streaks and your data. New to the game? Read how to play or the FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play ReverseMath?

Each puzzle shows a target number and an equation with blank slots. Tap or drag the number tiles into the blanks so the equation equals the target. Use each number once. Real math rules apply: multiplication and division are worked out before addition and subtraction. A quick intro in the app shows you the first time you open it.

What are the game modes?

Daily: five puzzles and three hearts (new players get a few extra hearts for their first Dailies), the same for everyone every day. It starts easy and gets harder across its five puzzles. A wrong answer costs a heart. Solve at least one puzzle to keep your streak, and see where you rank on today's leaderboard. If you run out of hearts you get one more chance, once per Daily: restart from the first puzzle (your restart score is lower).

Classic: five puzzles in four levels, from Novice to Genius. No countdown. Wrong answers add seconds, and every game shows your best time to beat.

Survival: a race against the clock. Start with a short clock and earn time with every puzzle you solve. The harder the puzzle, the more time you win. A wrong answer costs a few seconds.

Live Arena: the host opens an arena and shares the code. Everyone plays the same puzzles at the same time for 2 minutes, and the results appear right away.

Challenge Battle: send friends a code. They play the same puzzles whenever they like.

Live Arena and Challenge Battle are in the Battle tab.

How do Live Arena and Challenge Battle work?

Open the Battle tab.

Live Arena: open an arena and share the code or link. Up to 30 players can join. When at least 2 players are in, the host starts it. Everyone plays the same puzzles at the same time for 2 minutes. The host can remove a player or end the arena. If the host leaves, the arena closes.

Challenge Battle: start a battle, play first, then send the code or link by text, WhatsApp or any app. Friends play the same puzzles on a 2-minute clock, whenever they like, one try each. A battle stays open for 7 days, and the host can end it at any time. Turn on notifications to hear when someone beats your score.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

🌱 Novice: learn the basics
📚 Seasoned: a step up
⚡ Expert: for fast thinkers
🧠 Genius: hardcore mode

The Daily starts easy and gets harder across its five puzzles. In battles, the puzzles get harder as you go: a new level every 6 puzzles.

Why did a wrong answer cost me a heart or time?

In the Daily a wrong answer costs a heart. In Classic it adds seconds to your time. In Survival it costs a few seconds on the clock. Undo is free in every mode: place a number, and if it isn't right, undo it.

How do I earn and spend coins?

You earn coins by finishing the Daily, keeping your streak, finishing Classic and Survival games, and watching optional rewarded ads. Undo is free in every mode. In Classic you can spend coins on a Skip or a Hint. In Survival you can spend coins on a Hint or on extra seconds. The Daily, Live Arena and Challenge Battle have no hints or skips, so every player gets the same game. You can also buy coin packs in the Shop, on the Me tab.

How do I keep my streak?

Solve at least one Daily puzzle each day. Quitting the Daily ends it for today: no result, no coins and no streak for that day, and you cannot play it again. A new Daily starts tomorrow. Missing a day resets your streak. A daily reminder can help: it comes from the app, and you control it in your phone's notification settings.

Do I need an account? Can I delete my data?

No account or sign-up is needed. The app gives you a fun player name, which you can change. To delete your profile and its game data, go to Settings → Delete my profile in the app, or see how to delete your data.

Does the game need an internet connection?

Classic works without a connection. The Daily, Live Arena and Challenge Battle use the internet to send your result and show the leaderboard and battle boards. If you finish a Daily with no connection, the app keeps your result and sends it when you are back online. Ads and purchases also need a connection.

Can I play in a web browser?

Yes. Today's Daily and the Daily leaderboard are at play.reversemathpuzzle.com. There are no coins on the web.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Me tab, then the Shop, and choose Remove ads. This is a one-time purchase that removes the banner and full-screen ads, and the “Double with an ad” offer on result screens.

I purchased Remove Ads but ads are still showing.

Go to Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you used for the purchase. If the issue persists, contact us.

I found a bug or the app keeps crashing.

The fastest way to report a bug is from inside the app: go to Settings → Send feedback. It opens an email to us with your app version and platform filled in.

If you can't open the app at all, email support@reversemathpuzzle.com with your device model and OS version.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, not directly by us. For iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, find the order and tap Request a refund. For anything else, email us at support@reversemathpuzzle.com.

Pro tips

🔢 Order of operations matters

× and ÷ are worked out before + and −. Plan with the final result in mind, not left to right.

⚔️ Watch the score to beat

In a Challenge Battle, the bar under the title shows the score to beat.

📅 Daily first

The Daily is the same for everyone and keeps your streak alive. Do it before anything else.

↩️ Undo is free, use it

Undo is free in every mode. Not sure? Place a number and see if the equation gets closer. If not, undo and try another tile.
