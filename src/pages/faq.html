ReverseMath: frequently asked questions

A short list of quick answers about ReverseMath, the free math puzzle game.

The game

What is a reverse math puzzle?

A reverse math puzzle is one where you start from the answer. You see a target number and an equation with blanks. You place the given numbers so that the equation comes out to the target. See how to play for a worked example.

Do I need to be good at math?

You only need adding, subtracting, multiplying and dividing. The first level, Novice, is gentle, and you can undo a move in every mode.

Does the order of operations matter?

Yes. ReverseMath uses real math rules, so multiplying and dividing are worked out before adding and subtracting.

Modes and levels

How many puzzles are in the Daily?

Five, and they are the same for everyone. You have three hearts (new players get a few extra hearts for their first Dailies), and a wrong answer takes one. Read more about the daily math puzzle.

What are the four levels?

Novice, Seasoned, Expert and Genius. You can play any of them in Classic.

What is Survival?

A mode where you beat the clock. You start with a short timer, and you earn time for each puzzle you solve. Read about Survival mode.

Playing with friends

Can I play with friends?

Yes. In a Live Arena you play the same puzzles at the same time. In a Challenge Battle you send a code and friends play when they like.

Getting it

Is ReverseMath free?

Yes. It is free on iPhone and Android.

Can I play on a computer?

You can play today's puzzle in your web browser at play.reversemathpuzzle.com.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 15.3 or later, and Android 7.0 or later.
