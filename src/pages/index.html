How it plays

Learn it in ten seconds

A quick intro in the app shows you the first time.

1

See the target

Every puzzle shows a target number and an equation with blanks.

2

Fill the blanks

Tap or drag a number into each blank, using each one once. Real math rules apply: × and ÷ come before + and −.

3

Hit the target

Get it right and the next puzzle appears. In timed games a wrong answer costs you a few seconds, so think before you tap.
