Math brain teasers to try right now

These brain teasers are the same kind of puzzle you play in ReverseMath. Each one has a target, an equation with blanks and some numbers. Use each number once. The normal order of operations applies: × and ÷ come before + and −. Try them in your head, then open the answer.

How these teasers work

Look at the target first. Then find the × or ÷ sign. That is the best place to start.

Teaser 1: a warm-up

Target 11. Equation: blank × blank + blank. Numbers: 2, 3, 5.

Show the answer

2 × 3 + 5 = 6 + 5 = 11.

Teaser 2: take away

Target 10. Equation: blank × blank − blank. Numbers: 2, 3, 4.

Show the answer

4 × 3 − 2 = 12 − 2 = 10.

Teaser 3: divide first

Target 6. Equation: blank ÷ blank + blank. Numbers: 2, 3, 6.

Show the answer

6 ÷ 2 + 3 = 3 + 3 = 6. There is only one way that works.

Teaser 4: the order trap

Target 7. Equation: blank + blank × blank. Numbers: 1, 2, 3.

Show the answer

1 + 2 × 3 = 1 + 6 = 7. If you worked left to right you would get 9, so the order matters.

Teaser 5: four numbers

Target 9. Equation: blank + blank × blank − blank. Numbers: 2, 3, 4, 5.

Show the answer

2 + 3 × 4 − 5 = 2 + 12 − 5 = 9. There are other ways, too. Can you find one more?
