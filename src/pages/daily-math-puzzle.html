The daily math puzzle: five new puzzles every day

The ReverseMath Daily is a daily math puzzle you can finish with your morning coffee. Every day there are five new puzzles. They are the same for everyone, so you can compare notes with friends.

How the Daily works

Each puzzle gives you a target and an equation with blanks. Place the numbers so the equation comes out to the target. If you are new to this, read how to play first.

You have three hearts. A wrong answer takes one heart. New players get a few extra hearts for their first Dailies. Run out of hearts and you get one more chance, once per Daily: you restart from the first puzzle, and your restart score is lower. Your score has points for every puzzle you solve, plus a bonus for solving it fast. Wrong answers cost hearts, not points.

Everyone plays on equal terms. There are no hints in the Daily, so the leaderboard is fair. Undo is still there.

It starts easy

The Daily starts with an easier puzzle, so anyone can jump in. The later puzzles are harder.

Keep your streak and climb the board

Solve at least one puzzle and your streak grows by one day. Come back tomorrow to keep it going. After you play, see where you rank on today's leaderboard. Highest score wins, and a tie goes to the faster player.

Play today's puzzle

You can play the Daily in the free app on iPhone and Android. You can also play today's puzzle in your browser, with nothing to install.

If you want to take your time, try Classic (see the math puzzle game). If you want a race, try Survival, Live Arena or a Challenge Battle.
